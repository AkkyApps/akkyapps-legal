# Privacy Policy - AkkyApps

**Last updated: April 14, 2026**

AkkyApps ("we", "our") develops iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, and macOS applications as an individual developer. This privacy policy applies to all apps published under the AkkyApps name.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

All data you enter, such as text, settings, preferences, and app-specific content, is stored locally on your device or in Apple-provided services such as iCloud when enabled by the app or the system. It is not sent to external servers operated by AkkyApps.

## Third-Party Services

Our apps do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## In-App Purchases

Apps that include in-app purchases use Apple's StoreKit framework. Purchase transactions are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information.
