# Privacy Policy — AkkyApps

**Last updated: April 14, 2026**

AkkyApps ("we", "our") develops iOS applications as an individual developer.
This privacy policy applies to all apps published under the AkkyApps name.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

All data you enter (text, settings, preferences) is stored **locally on your device only**
using Apple's standard storage (UserDefaults / iCloud) and is never sent to external servers.

## Third-Party Services

Our apps do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## In-App Purchases

Apps that include in-app purchases use Apple's StoreKit framework.
Purchase transactions are processed entirely by Apple.
We do not receive or store your payment information.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us:

**Email:** akkyapps@icloud.com

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time.
Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
